    What would you like to swap?

    Tell me the day and what you're in the mood for. I'll choose from our recipes, keep your saved dietary settings, and update your shopping list.

    Try: "Swap Wednesday for something quicker" or "Replace Monday's dinner with something using spinach."

    Your request, active plan, pantry and dietary settings are sent to OpenAI to find a swap. Kept and finished dinners stay in place. A cooking-day swap also updates its reserved leftovers.
